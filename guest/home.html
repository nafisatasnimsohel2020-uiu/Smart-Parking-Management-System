General Parking Rules & Facility Guidelines

All visitors and guests must abide by our facility parking policies:

🚗 1. Valid Bay Markings

Vehicles must be parked strictly within marked stall lines. Blocking aisles, pedestrian pathways, or double-parking is strictly prohibited.

⏱️ 2. Speed Limits & Safety

Strict speed limit of 10 km/h across all facilities. Headlights must remain on in multi-level basements. Pedestrians always have the right of way.

⚡ 3. EV Charging Stations

Designated EV charging spots are reserved exclusively for electric vehicles undergoing active charging. Non-EV vehicles will be ticketed.

📱 4. Check-In & OTP Verification

Drivers must present their 4-digit booking OTP to the parking manager upon arrival. Overstaying past scheduled reservation incurs standard hourly charges.
